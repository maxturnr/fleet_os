Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 26, 2026

1. Information We Collect

FleetOS collects information you provide when you create an account and connect your QuickBooks account, including:

  • Email address and account credentials
  • QuickBooks financial data (transactions, accounts, vendors)
  • Vehicle inventory and transaction data you enter

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and maintain our service
  • Sync data between FleetOS and QuickBooks
  • Generate financial reports and analytics
  • Improve our service and user experience

3. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/SSL)
  • Secure database storage with Supabase
  • OAuth 2.0 for QuickBooks authentication

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties. We only share data with:

  • QuickBooks (as authorized by you)
  • Supabase (our secure database provider)

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction of your data
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Disconnect QuickBooks integration at any time

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@pierfront.co
